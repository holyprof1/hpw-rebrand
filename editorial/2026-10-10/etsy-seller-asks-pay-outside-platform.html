If an Etsy seller asks you to finish a purchase through a separate payment link or private chat, pause before paying. A listing on Etsy does not make a payment elsewhere an Etsy transaction. Check where the checkout actually happens and which protections apply.

Our Etsy marketplace explainer covers the platform. This article focuses on off-platform payment requests and messages that impersonate support.

Keep the proposed payment and the original order separate

Read the request carefully. Is the seller answering a product question, or asking you to replace the platform checkout with a different payment? A normal conversation about an item and a new request for money are not the same event. Do not let an existing order make every later payment request feel automatically legitimate.

Etsy's off-platform scam guidance warns that purchases moved away from its platform cannot rely on Etsy Purchase Protection. It tells buyers to avoid payment requests that bypass its checkout. That does not mean every on-platform order has an unconditional refund: the protection programme has its own eligibility rules.

Check the request inside the account

Open the Etsy app or type the known site address yourself, then inspect your messages and order. This gives you a separate path to the account instead of relying entirely on a link supplied by the sender. If the claimed problem or order cannot be located there, ask through the official account support route before taking action.

A support-looking name is not enough

Etsy's phishing guidance describes official messages in the From Etsy inbox with a corresponding badge. It also says Etsy will not ask for your password in Messages. Check those account indicators rather than trusting a sender who simply puts Etsy or Support into a display name.

Use the pressure test

Ask yourself what would happen if you waited long enough to check the account properly. A sender's urgency is not proof that the request is legitimate. Keep a record of the message and the proposed destination, but do not scan an unexpected code, share a verification code or enter account details on a page reached only through that message.

Report the message through the platform

For suspicious Messages, Etsy's guidance recommends reporting instead of replying. Use the reporting controls in your account. A useful private record includes the account name, the order it claimed to concern and a screenshot of the request. Remove private details before showing screenshots publicly.

What to check on your next order

  • The item, variation, price and delivery estimate match what you intended to buy.
  • The payment stays in the platform's authorised purchase flow.
  • Product questions and transaction evidence stay with the order.
  • You understand the applicable return and purchase-protection conditions.
