Free cancellation on Booking.com is a condition of the particular offer you book, not a single deadline applied to every hotel. Two rates for the same room can have different rules. The useful question is: what did the confirmation for my exact booking say?

Our Booking.com explainer covers the platform and its business model. This guide focuses on reading a cancellation policy.

Start with the rate, not the property headline

Before paying, inspect the chosen room and rate's conditions. Record whether cancellation is free, partly refundable or non-refundable, and the displayed deadline. Keep the date, time and any stated time zone together. “Free cancellation available” somewhere on a property page is not enough to establish the terms of a different rate.

Booking.com's customer terms say cancellation and no-show charges depend on the service provider's policy. They identify the booking flow, property information and confirmation as places those policies appear. The same hotel name therefore does not establish identical refund terms.

Save the confirmation before you need it

Keep the confirmation email or download a copy. A useful record includes the property, stay dates, room, rate terms and booking reference. Keep a screenshot of the displayed deadline if it helps. These records let you ask a precise question if the charge or cancellation outcome differs from what you expected.

Check whether it is a Partner Offer

Booking.com's Partner Offer help page describes offers facilitated through partner travel companies, with restrictions on changes. Read that section if your reservation carries the Partner Offer label. Its rules should not be applied to every ordinary reservation on the platform.

A practical check before buying is to ask whether your plans could change. If flexibility matters, compare the actual flexible offer and its final price instead of assuming customer support can later turn a restricted offer into one that allows changes.

Distinguish a request from confirmation

Use the cancellation route in the booking confirmation or your account, then retain the resulting cancellation acknowledgement. Booking.com's Partner Offer help page says a cancellation email should follow a completed cancellation. If the status is unclear, contact support through the official booking rather than assuming that an unanswered message to a property changed the reservation.

Do not substitute another hotel's refund timeline

Keep policy eligibility, cancellation confirmation and the arrival of money in your account as separate questions. Identify who collected the payment and use the support route relevant to your booking. We have not tested a refund, and there is no universal arrival date asserted here.

A short pre-booking record

  • Exact room and rate selected.
  • Cancellation deadline with its displayed time zone.
  • Fee after that deadline and no-show terms.
  • Payment collection details and any Partner Offer label.
  • A saved confirmation and official support route.

This checklist helps you avoid guessing from general advertising. For another platform's model, read our Agoda explainer and compare the actual offers rather than brand names alone.
